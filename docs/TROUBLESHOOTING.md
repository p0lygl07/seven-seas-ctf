# 7 Seas CTF — Troubleshooting

### The badge won't power on
Use a data/power USB cable and a known-good port. A charge-only cable may not enumerate.

### No UART output
Confirm 115200 baud, correct COM port, and TX/RX not swapped (TX↔RX).

### I'm stuck on a flag
Each solved flag reveals a hint for the next. Full hints publish here at launch.
